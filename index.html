Research profile

I am a physician and PhD candidate working at the intersection of machine learning, vascular medicine and cardiovascular prevention.

At LIMICS, Sorbonne Université and Inserm, I develop and evaluate approaches to clinical decision support through a CIFRE doctoral partnership with Zoī and collaboration with AP-HP. My work combines clinical data analysis, multimodal learning and evidence synthesis, informed by training in vascular medicine at Hôpital Européen Georges-Pompidou.

  1. Multimodal learning and model evaluation

    I study when integrating clinical, biological and imaging data improves prediction beyond a single modality. My first-author systematic review compares 97 studies across 12 medical specialties, with attention to methodological heterogeneity and risk of bias. It is currently available as a preprint.

  2. Prescribing behavior and synthetic clinical data

    I investigate how machine learning can represent cardiovascular prevention prescriptions and how within- and between-physician variability affects model evaluation. My 2026 first-author study compares real, synthetic and hybrid clinical datasets using cross-validation and held-out evaluation.

  3. Deep phenotyping for preventive medicine

    I contribute to research using the Zoī prospective cohort. Its published profile describes the first 1,000 participants, combining 196 biomarkers, more than 500 self-reported items and multimodal imaging to support research on early risk assessment and personalized prevention.
