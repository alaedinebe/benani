\section{Research Focus}
Physician and PhD candidate working on machine learning for cardiovascular prevention and clinical decision support. Research integrates multimodal modeling, synthetic clinical data and evidence synthesis, with particular attention to model interpretability, prescribing variability and rigorous evaluation.

\section{Research and Professional Experience}
\entry{Machine Learning Researcher and Doctoral Researcher}{May 2024--present}
LIMICS (Sorbonne Université / Inserm) and Zoī, Paris; CIFRE doctoral partnership.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Model cardiovascular prevention prescriptions using real, synthetic and hybrid clinical datasets; first-author study with cross-validation and held-out evaluation.
  \item Lead a systematic review comparing multimodal and unimodal models across 97 studies and 12 specialties, including PROBAST risk-of-bias assessment (preprint).
  \item Study within- and between-physician prescribing variability and its implications for model evaluation.
  \item Contribute to the Zoī prospective cohort: published profile of the first 1,000 participants, with 196 biomarkers, over 500 self-reported items and multimodal imaging.
\end{itemize}
\entry{Medical Project Manager / Data Consultant}{April 2020--February 2024}
Synapse Medicine and independent collaborations, including Raidium. Contributed to Goodmed, Synapse Pro, the Synapse Copilot knowledge base and medical question-answering research (ClinicalNLP 2024).

Owkin, Paris, France. Master's research internship in machine learning for CT image analysis within the radiology team.
